Cosmos.Network.Http embeds third-party material:

resources/cacert.pem
    Mozilla's root certificates (CA bundle), as extracted by the curl project
    from Mozilla's certdata.txt: https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html
    Embedded unmodified in the assembly as the resource
    "Cosmos.Network.Http.cacert.pem".
    License: Mozilla Public License, version 2.0
    https://www.mozilla.org/MPL/2.0/
    Source: https://hg.mozilla.org/mozilla-central/raw-file/tip/security/nss/lib/ckfw/builtins/certdata.txt

The package also depends on, without embedding it:

BouncyCastle.Cryptography
    https://github.com/bcgit/bc-csharp
