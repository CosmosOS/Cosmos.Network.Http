    Mozilla's root certificates (CA bundle), as extracted by the curl project
    from Mozilla's certdata.txt: https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html
    Embedded unmodified in the assembly as the resource
    "Cosmos.Network.Http.cacert.pem".
